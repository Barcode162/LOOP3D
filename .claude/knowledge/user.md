## How to talk to them
- Plain words, short, the important thing first. Say **what to look at on the
  phone** ("open the bar, stand by the fire, the neon should buzz"), never "it
  should work".
- One question per real decision, **with a recommendation first** (they almost
  always pick "(Recommended)"). Never a menu of technical options.
- Translate tech into what they'll see. No file names unless they ask.
- Never say done or live without checking. Own every mistake plainly.
- They love ambition and "wow" ("UNITY KILLER 9001", "go above and beyond",
  "scientific breakthrough", "THE single best answer"), but **"1 mistake is worse
  than making sure you are doing the right thing"**: research first, then build.
- They get frustrated by **waiting** (slow tests, deploy queues, idle Claude) and
  by fixes that keep missing ("I've tried a lot of times it doesn't seem like
  we've ever found the right fix").
- "go" = continue the plan. "broke X" = that change broke something. "undo",
  "status", "park: …", "talk" (conversation only).

## What they consistently want (their taste)
- **Our own everything:** engine, language, tools, models, from scratch.
- **Real, and alive:** real light, physics, sound, pushed livelier than life; the
  world moves on its own (their "loops" idea, like FL Studio patterns).
- **Lighting that looks truly real**, like the best modern games ("I've seen get so
  fucking real I want that"). They keep learning and starting over with new
  systems to get there. Effects like bloom are fine **as adjustable settings**

  of that build, not rules (origin-3d/lighting-story.md is history, not a spec).
- **Smooth on the phone first.** Quality levels (Low/Medium/High/Ultra), power
  modes (Cooler/Balanced/EXTREME).
- **Ember** look (amber, frosted glass, glow, moving titles) for UI; constant
  smooth animation; not cluttered.
- **Licensed assets only**, credited; no copyrighted music.
- **13+ platform**, teens and adults apart; casino 18+ with earned credits only.
