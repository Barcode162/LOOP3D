# The vision (3D's North star and the product)

## North star (the user's five lines, word for word)
- **N1 A world you're inside.** joshuavelasco.com is one place, not a set of pages: the bar, the showcase and Dev are places in it, joined by movement.
- **N2 Made from scratch.** Our own engine, Looped, and our own script language: no outside engines. Made by J Production.
- **N3 Real, and alive.** Real light, shapes, materials, physics and sound, pushed livelier than real life: the world keeps moving on its own; joining in is optional and fun. Engines that stack: each one makes the others better.
- **N4 A life with other people.** A hangout for 13+: homes, games, events, credits, voice; safe by design.
- **N5 Made on the iPhone.** Everything is judged on the user's phone (Safari): smooth first, one look (the house theme Ember) everywhere.

## "Simulate, don't script" (2026-10-15)
Build a world simulation, not props: engines for every sense whose behaviour
*emerges*. One material = every sense (look, physics, sound, how it breaks).
Sound generated from physics; room echo from shape and materials; later heat,
smell as a field, day/night, wear and dirt where things are used.

## The product: a virtual life in the browser (2026-10-19)
Start at your **home** → jump to a **few main hubs** (the bar first) → play,
watch or queue → earn **credits** (own 3D coin; valued like a dollar; 5–50 a
game, daily caps) → grow your home and look. Lobbies by age band, preference and
skill; live pixelated machine screens; a fair 20 s queue; "Happening now"; a
no-friends lobby. Homes: invite-only, grid placement, expand 3×, levels 1–3.
An IKEA-style mall (walk, cart, delivery). Casino 18+, earned credits only.
VIP marketplace for real money (numbered, seasonal, event items; first purchase
unlocks its door forever). Trading only for tradeable items. Events with RSVP.
A memory book (in-game camera, likes, no comments). Voice first; teens get a
phrase builder. Later: a street and a city outside.

## Loops (the user's own idea, the engine's identity, 2026-10-20)
Like FL Studio: every thing plays its own pattern on **one shared clock locked
to the music**, so the world is alive on its own. Later wished as a **Loop
Maker**, a DAW/video-editor timeline of clips over things (2026-10-25 #5).

## Looped Script (2026-10-27 #10, #11)
"Every word is a script": words with real-world meaning, a web of connections,
describing words tweak things, cause-and-effect words spread change, all ending
as the numbers of the world's maths. Build the skeleton first, as perfected labs.
