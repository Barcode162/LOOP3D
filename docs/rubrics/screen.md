# Rubric: a screen

**Use it for:** a phone screenshot with the game's screens on it (bars, counters, goals, buttons, a bag, a
menu), held upright unless the order says wide. The lead says which thumb zones the phone has (the bottom
third and the edges) and what the player is meant to be doing at that moment.

**The reference, in words** (`docs/CRAFT.md`, section 11; `docs/MECHANICS_UI.md`).
The player always knows three things: what to do, how they are doing, and what just changed. Thumbs reach
the bottom third and the edges; the top is for reading. One hand is enough for most things. Buttons are 44
points or more, with space between. Screens say less: what is full or unchanged hides, numbers show only
when they matter, words sit under icons until learned. Each role lives in its own place (vital top left,
resource top right, pressure top centre, the goal in one line under it, actions in an arc bottom right with
the main one largest, the stick bottom left). The world stays the picture: the screens sit round its edges
and never over what the player is looking at. Everyone can play: text large enough, strong contrast, colour
never the only signal.

## The ten questions

1. **What to do.** In two seconds, can you say what the player should do now?
2. **How you are doing.** In the same two seconds, can you say how they are doing (health, time, count)?
3. **What just changed.** Is the last change marked (a flash, a pop, a tick) and gone again soon after?
4. **Thumb reach.** Is everything you press in the bottom third or at the edges, the main action under the
   right thumb, and nothing to press at the top?
5. **Size and spacing.** Is every button 44 points or more, with room between so a thumb cannot hit two?
6. **Says less.** Is anything shown that is full, unchanged or not needed now? Any number that does not
   matter?
7. **Reading.** Is text large enough at arm's length, with contrast against whatever is behind it, and is
   colour never the only signal?
8. **One family.** Do all the pieces share one theme: shapes, corners, type, icons, spacing?
9. **The world stays the picture.** Is the middle of the screen clear, and does anything cover what the
   player needs to see?
10. **Cleanliness.** Nothing overlapping, nothing cut off, nothing under the notch or the home bar, no
    misaligned edges.
