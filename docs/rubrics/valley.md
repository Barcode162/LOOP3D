# Rubric: The Valley

**Use it for:** milestone 6, the landscapes: the turquoise river, the towers in mist, the plateau falls, the
terraces above the clouds, and the painted town on the falls.

**The reference, in words** (`docs/CRAFT.md`, section 1, "The landscapes").
Scale is told by something small: terraces and ruins on a ridge, boats on the river, a road, trees on a cliff
edge. The air can be seen: each ridge further away is paler and bluer, mist lies in the low places with a
flat top, and the sun comes through gaps as shafts. Water made the land: every valley drains somewhere;
cliffs stand where the rock is hard and slopes lie where it is soft; karst towers stand steep-sided and
round-topped in a flat plain; falls sit on the lip of a hard layer and break from a sheet into threads, then
spray. The water's colour has a reason: turquoise is clear water over pale stones with fine rock dust in it,
dark green is depth and the forest's reflection, white is air mixed in where it is steep. Growth obeys the
land: forest to the tree line, moss in the spray of the falls, bare rock on the steepest faces, fields only
where it is flat. The sky is half the picture: clouds lit from below at sunset, a break for the sun. And
three depths: a dark near thing, the subject, the far haze.
*The painted town adds:* one strong roof colour repeated, buildings that grow out of the rock, bridges
tying islands together, oversized plants, and mist hiding where the land ends.

## The ten questions

1. **Light.** One sun, low enough to show the land's shape, with long shadows; a sky that lights the shade
   blue; clouds lit from the sun's side.
2. **Surfaces.** At the nearest bank or rock: wet stone darker and shinier than dry, moss where the spray
   reaches, pale rounded stones under the shallows.
3. **Edges and contact.** Does water meet its bank with a wet line and a little foam, do trees stand in the
   ground, do cliffs have scree at their feet?
4. **Detail in three sizes.** The range; the cliffs, gullies and benches; the stones, plants and ripples
   near you.
5. **Depth.** A dark near thing, the subject, then ridges fading: can you count the ridges by their tone?
6. **Scale.** Is there something small that tells the size (a boat, a terrace, a road, a tree on the edge),
   and does it make the mountain large?
7. **Variety.** Does the land look made by water (valleys that branch and drain, no dents without an
   outlet), does growth sort itself by height, water and slope with ragged edges, and does anything repeat?
8. **The water's colour and clearness.** Looking down do you see the bed, looking along do you see the
   sky? Is the colour the right one for its depth and its bed, white only where it is steep?
9. **Haze layers and shafts.** Does mist lie low with a flat top, do far ridges go pale and blue in clear
   steps, and do shafts appear only where there is mist or trees for the sun to cut through?
10. **Cleanliness.** No hard line where water meets land, no shimmer on far forest, no banding in the
    mist, no seam between near and far land.

Questions 8 and 9 stand in place of the base's colour and life; life moved into question 6 (the small thing
that tells the size is nearly always a sign of people).
