# Rubric: any picture (the base)

**Use it for:** any render or screenshot that has no rubric of its own; every library entry (its three seeds);
every old scene. The star rubrics keep its questions 1 to 7 and 10 and change 8 and 9.

**The reference, in words.** A photograph of a real place taken by someone who cares: one clear source of
light and the shade it leaves; things made of something you could name by touch; every edge a little rounded
and sitting in its own small shadow; large shapes, parts and small things all present; something near,
something far and air between; a thing whose size you know; nothing lined up that a person or nature would
not line up; a few colours that belong together; a sign that someone was just here; and no mark of the
machine that made it.

## The ten questions

1. **Light.** Can you point at where each light comes from (a window, a lamp, the sun, a screen), and is the
   contrast right for the mood? *Low:* even grey light with no source, lifted shadows. *High:* dark is dark,
   bright things are few and have a reason.
2. **Surfaces.** At the nearest thing, do you believe what it is made of? *Low:* one flat colour, plastic
   shine. *High:* slow change, fine change and grain between; shine that varies; true brightness.
3. **Edges and contact.** Do things sit on what they touch, with rounded edges and a shadow where they meet?
   *Low:* knife edges, things that float or sink. *High:* every edge catches a line of light, every foot
   has its dark seam.
4. **Detail in three sizes.** Are big forms, medium parts and small things each present? *Low:* only big
   (a toy) or only small (noise). *High:* the eye finds something new at each distance.
5. **Depth.** Is there a near, a middle and a far, and air between them? *Low:* everything at one distance,
   equally sharp and equally coloured. *High:* a dark near thing, the subject, a paler far.
6. **Scale.** Is there something that tells the size, and is everything the size it should be? *Low:* no
   clue, or a brick the size of a book. *High:* a door, a chair, a person, a plant you know.
7. **Variety.** Does anything repeat or line up in a way nature or a home would not? *Low:* the same rock
   five times, an even grid of trees, a pattern that tiles. *High:* groups and gaps, old beside young.
8. **Colour.** A few colours that belong together, and one accent? *Low:* every colour at full strength, or
   all one grey. *High:* two or three families, one small thing that sings.
9. **Life.** Any sign that something lives or has just happened here? *Low:* a showroom. *High:* a person,
   an animal, a track, a thing left where it was used.
10. **Cleanliness.** No flicker marks, holes, hard seams, grain, glowing edges, popping or banding.
    *Low:* any of them where the eye lands. *High:* none anywhere.
