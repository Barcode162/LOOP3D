# Rubric: a person

**Use it for:** one person in a picture, from full body to a close-up; also a creature (read "paws" for feet).

**The reference, in words** (`docs/CRAFT.md`, section 8).
The look is *stylised real*: the people of a good animated film, never a failed photograph. Slightly larger
eyes and hands, simpler skin, clear shapes. A body about seven and a half heads tall; elbows at the waist,
wrists at the hips, fingertips at mid-thigh; eyes halfway down the head. Nobody stands symmetrical: the
weight is on one leg, the hips tilt one way and the shoulders the other. The eyes lead: they are wet, they
catch a light, they look *at* something, and the head and body follow them. A smile reaches the eyes. A
little asymmetry is life. Skin lets light in (ears and fingers glow from behind); hair has strands and a
shape; clothes hang from the shoulders, crease at the joints and rest on the body. For a person: a key light
30 to 45 degrees to one side and above, fill at a quarter to an eighth of it, a rim from behind to cut them
from the background.

## The ten questions

1. **Light on the person.** Is there a key, a softer fill and a rim that parts them from the background, each
   with a reason in the scene?
2. **Skin, hair and cloth.** Do you believe each: skin soft and a little see-through at the ears, hair with
   strands and a sheen, cloth with a weave and a fold?
3. **Edges and contact.** Do clothes rest on the body, hair on the shoulders, hands on what they hold, with
   small shadows where they meet and nothing passing through anything?
4. **Detail in three sizes.** The silhouette; the features, hands and folds; lashes, nails, seams, buttons.
5. **Proportion.** Head count, limb lengths, the size of hands and feet: right for this age and build?
6. **Pose.** Readable as a black shape? Weight on one leg, hips and shoulders countered, nothing mirrored?
7. **The face.** Does it hold an expression you can name, a little uneven, the mouth and the eyes agreeing?
8. **The eyes.** Wet, lit by a small catch-light, looking at one thing, with lids that fit the look? At a
   close-up this is the question that decides.
9. **Weight on the feet.** Do the feet press flat on the ground with a contact shadow, is the body balanced
   over them, does a sitter sink into the seat?
10. **Cleanliness.** No skin showing through cloth, no collapsed elbow or shoulder, no floating hair, no
    dead stare; and never the nearly-real face that looks dead (if it is there, say so first).
