# Rubric: a move

**Use it for:** one move (a walk, a turn, a sit, a jump, a reach, a fall, an idle), judged from a strip of
frames. The lead gives the judge one picture holding eight or more evenly spaced frames from the side, a
second from the front, the time between frames, and a line on the ground under the feet.

**The reference, in words** (`docs/CRAFT.md`, section 8).
Recorded human motion, played on the standard skeleton. A walk is about two steps a second at 1.4 metres a
second: the hips drop on the passing leg, the shoulders counter the hips, the head stays nearly level, the
foot lands heel first and never slides. A run has a moment with no foot down. A turn starts with the eyes,
then the head, then the body. Before a big move comes a small one the other way. Nothing starts or stops at
full speed. Parts arrive at different times: hair, cloth and arms trail and settle. Joints swing, so hands
and heads travel in curves. Heavy things start slowly, stop slowly and make the body lean. Idle is never
still: breathing, a shift of weight every 5 to 15 seconds, a glance.

## The ten questions

1. **A start, a peak and a settle.** Does the move prepare (a small move the other way), reach one clear
   peak, then come to rest with a little overshoot?
2. **Feet that do not slide.** Against the ground line: does a planted foot stay exactly where it landed
   until it lifts? Any slide at all is 4 or less.
3. **Weight.** Do the hips drop and the knees give on landing, does the body lean against what is heavy, is
   the balance over the feet in every frame?
4. **Timing.** Is the speed a person's (two steps a second walking), with slow in and slow out, and frames
   bunched where it is slow?
5. **Arcs.** Do the hands, the head and the feet travel in curves from frame to frame, never straight lines
   or zigzags?
6. **Overlap.** Do parts arrive at different times: hips, then chest, then head; the arm after the shoulder;
   hair and cloth last?
7. **Counter-turn.** Do the shoulders turn against the hips, the arms against the legs, and does the head
   stay nearly level?
8. **The eyes lead.** In a turn or a reach, do the eyes go first, then the head, then the body?
9. **Silhouette.** Is every key pose readable as a black shape, with the limbs clear of the body?
10. **Cleanliness.** No pop between frames, no knee or elbow snapping straight, no limb passing through the
    body, no foot under the ground, no shake.
