# Echoes of Ardane

> *"Everyone you come from is still walking somewhere. You just have to go and meet them."*

A story-driven game made with Loop3D. This is the director's script: the story bible (Part A), all 24 scenes (Part B) and the epilogue (Part C). Every scene is built as a Looped Script file in this folder, and its file name sits next to its title.

---

# PART A — THE STORY BIBLE

## 1. Title, pitch, themes

**Echoes of Ardane**

**Pitch:** A shy, poor boy running from the richest family in the city ducks into an alley and falls through time. In five worlds he walks in the footsteps of the ancestors his family forgot. Each one hands him a piece of godlike power, and each one asks whether he can carry it without forgetting who he is.

**Themes**
- **Dignity has no price.** It is kept or lost, never bought.
- **Power makes you forget.** The more doors that open, the easier it is to forget which one was home.
- **Where you come from is a root, not a cage.** Roots are what let a tree stand in a storm.
- **The poor and the powerful are the same people, just in different weather.** Every world shows both sides, and gives both their dignity.
- **Honor the fallen; love the past, present and future at once.** Moving on is not leaving behind.

**Tone:** Quiet and patient, with sudden heights. Long held shots, rain on windows, small hands doing small things. Then, when it matters, the sky opens.

## 2. The main character

**Milo Ardane**, fourteen.

- **His family:** he lives with his mother and grandmother and his seven-year-old sister in one room above a laundry on Calder Row, in the low city.
- **How he looks:** thin, with a navy hooded jacket two sizes too big that was his father's. His hair falls in his eyes. He walks close to walls.
- **His voice:** he speaks rarely and softly. He thinks in full paragraphs and says one sentence. When he does speak it's usually a question, or the truest thing in the room. He hums his grandmother's well-song without knowing it. He counts things when he's scared: steps, coins, breaths.
- **His fears:**
  - being seen
  - being seen *as nothing*
  - his mother's tired hands
  - the day the room above the laundry is taken away
- **What he wants:** money, enough that his mother can sleep, and respect, enough that no one spits on the Ardane name again. He wants to matter.
- **What he needs:** to understand that he already matters, and that the respect worth having starts with his own. He needs to learn to hold power without letting it hold him, and to grieve and still move forward.

**How he changes in every world**

| World | What he learns | What it costs him | What we see |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home (Calder Row) | Shame, and quiet stubbornness | — | Hood up, eyes down |
| Velasse (water) | Courage, from Nia; that the poor can save the rich | The weight of every drowned name | His posture rises; he starts looking people in the eye |
| Qadesh (sand) | The view from the palace; that the powerful are afraid too | Thirst for *more* | He gives orders for the first time, and likes it a little too much |
| Aelor (sky) | Being envied, and being lonely at the top | His roots; he forgets his sister's birthday | He wears a noble's coat over his father's jacket |
| Nexus 9 (cyber) | Seeing the code of things; time slowing | Feeling; everything becomes numbers | His eyes faintly glow; he speaks in calculations |
| The Hollow | Getting everything, forever | Himself | The coat is gold; the jacket is gone |
| The end | That power is a tool, not a home | A scar: a white streak in his hair, a tremor in his left hand, and a silence he'll always carry | Hood down; the jacket is back, mended with red thread |

## 3. His family

**Today**
- **Rosa Ardane** (39), his mother.
  - Works nights at the laundry and days cleaning the Varen offices.
  - Burnt hands, a laugh she rations, proud in a way that costs her.
  - She stopped saying the family name aloud the year Milo's father left.
- **Bea Ardane** (7), his sister.
  - Fearless, loud, draws on every surface.
  - Draws Milo as a giant with a lantern.
- **Nana Ines Ardane** (81), his grandmother.
  - Bedridden. Hands always busy with red thread.
  - She tells stories nobody believes and sings a well-song in a language nobody else speaks.
  - She ties **the Knot**, a red-thread bracelet, onto Milo's wrist on the first morning. She knows exactly what it is.
- **His father,** gone. A name on an envelope with no letter inside.

**The ancestors behind each relic**

| Relic | Ancestor | World | Their feat |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Knot** (the thread) | Nana Ines herself | Home | She kept the family's memory alive in thread and song when the world took everything else. The first relic, and the last one he understands. |
| **The Tide Compass** | Seren Ardane, a ferrywoman | Velasse, ~1490 | In the great flood, she opened the Great Sluice so the water would drown the nobles' gardens instead of the poor quarter. Hanged for it; remembered by no one. |
| **The Brass Key of Wells** | Tobiah Ardane, a well-digger | Qadesh, ~1200 BCE | He found the Deep Well beneath the city and gave its water to everyone, free. The Sunborn sealed the well and erased his name from the walls. |
| **The Seed of Lune** | Lune Ardane, a gardener-engineer | Aelor, a Victorian-era garden kingdom | When the war-engine's blast tore the islands from the earth, she grew roots of living iron to stop them falling apart, and was cursed to float with them forever. |
| **The Mercy Shard** | Ada Ardane, a programmer | Nexus 9, ~2310 | She wrote the Mercy Protocol, the line of code that ended the drone wars. The Helix Consortium deleted her from every record and kept the credit. |

## 4. The worlds

The same spot, **Thirteenth Alley** in Calder Row, exists in every world. The portal always opens there. Every world is a different age of human history and a different way people have ruled each other.

### Home — Calder Row (the present)
- **Era and rule:** a modern city run by old money. Officially a democracy; in practice ruled by the families who own the buildings, the market and the council votes.
- **Great family:** **the Varens.**
  - Augustin Varen owns Calder Row, the market arcade and the debt.
  - His son Julian (16) is beautiful, bored and cruel.
- **The poor:** two jobs, one room, a list of what's owed.
- **The rich:** glass towers on the hill, lit all night.
- **Look:**
  - brick, wet asphalt, sodium-orange streetlights, laundry steam
  - neon pharmacy crosses, low clouds, constant drizzle
  - the hill's towers glittering above it all
- **Sound:** rain, laundry machines, distant traffic, a radio two floors down.
- **Lesson:** where he starts. Shame is a weight, but also a root.

### World of Water — Velasse (a Renaissance merchant republic)
- **Era and rule:** a drowned city of towers and canals, ruled by an oligarchy of merchant Doges. Votes are sold by the boat-load.
- **Great family:** **House Veyra**, the Canal Lords. Their gardens sit on the only dry ground.
- **The poor:** the Low Water folk live on lashed rafts beneath the palaces, ferrying the rich for coins thrown, never handed.
- **The rich:** high gardens, bells, silk awnings, glass lanterns.
- **Look:**
  - green-black water; stone towers rising out of it, stained to the waterline; rope bridges
  - lantern light; morning mist that turns gold
  - and in the storm scene, black rain and white water
- **Sound:** water lapping, bells, gulls, oars, singing across the canals.
- **Lesson:** courage, and that the low can save the high.

### World of Sand — Qadesh (a Bronze Age desert empire)
- **Era and rule:** a theocracy. The **Sunborn dynasty** are god-kings who own every well. Water is law; thirst is obedience.
- **The poor:** queue at dawn at the royal cisterns for a cup each.
- **The rich:** fountains that run for beauty. Their courtyards are cool and green behind sandstone walls.
- **The underground:** **the Cistern Brotherhood**, militant water-rebels who live in the abandoned tunnels under the city. They distrust everyone.
- **Look:**
  - white noon sun, red sandstone, linen, heat haze, palm shade, brass
  - and in the storm scene, an orange dusk under a wall of sand
- **Sound:** wind over dunes, copper bells, a market crowd, the deep drip of the tunnels.
- **Lesson:** the view from the palace. The powerful are afraid too. And how it feels to give orders.

### World of Green Sky — Aelor (a Victorian garden kingdom)
- **Era and rule:** an aristocracy of **Gilded Houses**, with a parliament of lords. Industry is run on the backs of the "rootless."
- **The curse:** a weapon from Nexus 9's future war tore the land out of the earth, and the islands have hung in the sky ever since, unable to come down.
- **Great family:** **House Ashcombe**, who keep the high gardens.
- **The poor:** the rootless, chain-workers who hang beneath the islands and tend the living roots that hold them together.
- **The rich:** glasshouses, waterfalls pouring into nothing, white tea at sunset.
- **Look:**
  - green islands with waterfalls spilling into clouds; white-glass greenhouses
  - iron tethers, birch and willow
  - golden hour almost always; at night, the stars below you as well as above
- **Sound:** wind, birdsong, waterfalls, distant chains, string quartets.
- **Lesson:** loneliness at the top, and the temptation to belong to the wrong people.

### The Future — Nexus 9 (a corporate technocracy)
- **Era and rule:** **the Helix Consortium** runs the city as a company. Citizenship is a subscription; every face is scanned.
- **Its role:** the **drone wars** started here, and their weapons cursed Aelor's sky.
- **The poor:** the Undercity, called **Static** by its people: cables, steam, food stalls, hand-built light.
- **The rich:** the Helix Tower, silent white floors above the rain.
- **Look:**
  - neon magenta and cyan in black rain, holographic ads, chrome and wet concrete
  - drones blinking red; the tower lit white like a hospital
- **Sound:** rain on metal, the hum of power, drone whine, synth music bleeding from stalls.
- **Lesson:** seeing everything as information, and the danger of losing feeling.

### The Hollow (outside time)
- **Era and rule:** none. A perfect city that is the shape of every wish. Milo is king, and everyone smiles.
- **The catch:** it never gets to tomorrow.
- **Look:** gold and white, too clean, sun fixed at noon, shadows that don't move.
- **Sound:** applause that never stops, then silence.
- **Lesson:** success without an ending is a cage.

## 5. The companions

**Nia Calla** (15), from Velasse
- **Who she is:** a Low Water ferry girl and smuggler. Quick, funny, fierce. She sings across the canals.
- **What she gives him:** courage, and laughter. She teaches him to look people in the eye ("They can't drown you with a look, Milo").
- **What she costs him:** she follows him through every world. When he loses himself in the Hollow, it's her voice he forgets first, and remembering it is how he comes back. At the end she goes home to Velasse.

**Oren Sa-Teph** (38), from Qadesh
- **Who he is:** a palace guard of the Sunborn who has started to doubt, and a deserter by the end of Qadesh. Big, slow-spoken, a soldier's honesty.
- **What he gives him:** the elite side of the story. Oren grew up in the palace and knows that kings cry, and that cruelty is usually fear.
- **What he costs him:** Oren takes the wound meant for Milo in the battle and loses the use of his sword arm.

**Sela Ashcombe** (17), from Aelor
- **Who she is:** a noble daughter of House Ashcombe, brilliant, educated, sharp-tongued. She sees through Milo's disguise in a second.
- **What she gives him:** a mirror of the rich who chooses to stand with the poor. She teaches him that dignity isn't a class.
- **What she costs him:** she gives up her title and her family to follow him, and her father disowns her in front of everyone.

**PIP-4, "Pip"**, from Nexus 9
- **Who he is:** a battered maintenance robot the size of a dog, with one cracked lens. He speaks in error codes he translates into feelings ("ERROR 404: dignity not found. That means I'm embarrassed").
- **What he gives him:** memory. Pip stores everything, and in the end he gives Milo back the moments he lost.
- **What he costs him:** Pip falls in the battle. Honoring him is the last lesson.

## 6. Allies, rivals, factions, and the final opponent

**Allies**
- **Old Ezio**, Nia's grandfather, a raft-builder. The first person in any world to speak the name Ardane with respect.
- **Mother Hesk**, leader of the Cistern Brotherhood: a cold, scarred woman who becomes an ally once Milo sings the well-song.
- **The rootless chain-workers** of Aelor, led by a quiet woman called **Bram.**
- **Juno-7,** a Static rebel netrunner in Nexus 9.

**Rivals**
- **Julian Varen** (home): the bully who is himself afraid of his father. Their final meeting, in the epilogue, is quiet.
- **Doge Aurelio Veyra** (Velasse), who orders the flood gates locked.
- **High Priest Ankh-Ra** (Qadesh).
- **Lord Percival Ashcombe** (Aelor), Sela's father.
- **Director Kade of the Helix Consortium** (Nexus 9).

**The underground factions**

| Faction | World | Who they are | How Milo gets in |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Low Water | Velasse | Rafts and smugglers | They take him in from the start |
| The Cistern Brotherhood | Qadesh | Water-rebels | They hate outsiders, so he has to infiltrate them, proving himself with the well-song and his knowledge of water |
| The Root Warrens | Aelor | The rootless chain-workers | — |
| Static | Nexus 9 | The Undercity | He infiltrates by decrypting Ada Ardane's old code |

### The final opponent: **Veyl**

- **Who he is:** a boy once, a thousand years ago. Poor, like Milo, in a Calder Row that was still a village. He found the portal first. He gathered the relics of *his* ancestors and became the first king of every world.
- **What he did with it:** every great family in every world, from Veyra to the Sunborn, Ashcombe, Helix and the Varens, traces its power back to Veyl. He planted them. He decided that only the strong deserve to be safe. The war that cursed Aelor's sky was his, fought against the one person who threatened his order: **Ada Ardane**.
- **His real motive:** he can't stop. His mother sent him out for bread one winter night and he came home to find her gone. Taken by the cold, by debt, by the landlord, he never knew which. Since then he has built a world where nothing can be taken from *him*, ever. That means owning everything.
- **Why he's Milo's dark mirror:** same alley, same hunger, same love for a mother. Veyl took the relics and forgot his roots. Milo takes them and remembers. Veyl lives in the Hollow; the Hollow is *his* mind, and Milo nearly becomes him there.
- **What his mind looks like inside:**
  - an endless palace of mirrors and gold doors
  - every mirror shows Veyl crowned
  - every door opens onto another throne room
  - at the very center, behind the last door, a single cold room: a straw bed, a cold hearth, a boy of ten sitting with a loaf of bread in his lap, waiting for someone who isn't coming
  - the snow outside the window has been falling for a thousand years

## 7. The relics

### 1. The Knot (Home)
- **Ancestor:** Nana Ines. She kept the family together and kept its songs.
- **Trial:** none. It's given freely, which is the point.
- **Power:** it brings him back. Across worlds, out of the Hollow, out of his own head. It always knows the way home.
- **Price:** none. Love asks only to be remembered.
- **How it changes him:** it doesn't, not until the Hollow, where it's the only thing that does.

### 2. The Tide Compass (Velasse)
- **Ancestor's feat:** Seren opened the Great Sluice in the flood of 1490 to save the Low Water.
- **Trial:** in the storm, Milo must climb the sluice tower and turn the iron wheel against the Veyra guards while the poor quarter fills with water.
- **Power:** command of water and calm. Waves still; rain stops around him.
- **Price:** he hears the drowned. Every name of everyone the flood ever took.
- **How it changes him:** he stands straighter. He's no longer afraid of being seen.

### 3. The Brass Key of Wells (Qadesh)
- **Ancestor's feat:** Tobiah found the Deep Well and gave it to everyone.
- **Trial:** in the sandstorm, Milo must descend the sealed Deep Well, find the stone door, and open it with the well-song.
- **Power:** finding what is hidden: water, ways, lies, the shape of a door in a wall.
- **Price:** thirst. Not for water, for *more*.
- **How it changes him:** he gives orders. He's good at it. It frightens Nia.

### 4. The Seed of Lune (Aelor)
- **Ancestor's feat:** Lune grew the iron roots that hold the shattered islands together.
- **Trial:** in the Root Warrens, as the roots fail, Milo must reach Lune's root-engine at the bottom of the islands and plant the Seed in it.
- **Power:** growth and flight. Things live and rise at his touch; he can walk on air.
- **Price:** rootlessness. The higher he goes, the less he remembers the ground.
- **How it changes him:** he starts dressing like the Ashcombes. He forgets Bea's birthday.

### 5. The Mercy Shard (Nexus 9)
- **Ancestor's feat:** Ada wrote the Mercy Protocol and ended the drone war.
- **Trial:** inside the Helix archive core, Milo must restore Ada's deleted code before the Consortium wipes it forever, while the drones close in.
- **Power:** the code of things. He can see how anything is made, and slow time almost to stopping.
- **Price:** feeling. Faces become data; tears become numbers.
- **How it changes him:** his eyes glow. He calls Pip "it."

## 8. The words of the final mind-scene

*In Veyl's mind. The cold room at the center. The boy Veyl, ten, holds the bread. Milo kneels so their eyes are level. He takes his time.*

> **Milo:** I know this room.
>
> **Milo:** Mine has a laundry under it. Yours has snow.
>
> *(a long silence; the snow keeps falling)*
>
> **Milo:** You went out for bread. And when you came back, she was gone. And you've been holding it ever since. A thousand years of holding bread for someone who isn't coming.
>
> **Young Veyl:** If I put it down, it means she's really gone.
>
> **Milo:** I know.
>
> **Milo:** I thought if I got strong enough, nothing could be taken from me. You thought that too. That's why you took everything first.
>
> **Milo:** But I went where you went. I had it all. A city that clapped forever. And I forgot my sister's name. I forgot my own.
>
> **Milo:** It wasn't the power that was wrong. It was that I stopped walking.
>
> *(he looks at the Knot on his wrist)*
>
> **Milo:** My grandmother says you don't carry the people you lose. You let them carry you. The ones before us walk ahead, and they leave the light on.
>
> **Milo:** So here's what I learned. It's not much. I'm not very good at speeches.
>
> **Milo:** You keep your head up. Even when it's heavy. *Especially* when it's heavy.
>
> **Milo:** You keep growing. Even when it hurts, even when no one claps.
>
> **Milo:** You don't betray yourself. Not for gold. Not for safety. Not even to stop hurting.
>
> **Milo:** You honor the ones who fell. You say their names. Out loud.
>
> **Milo:** And you love it. All of it. What was. What is. What's coming. You love it so much you can let it go.
>
> *(he holds out his open hand, under the bread)*
>
> **Milo:** You can put it down now. She'd want you to eat.
>
> *(Young Veyl looks at the bread. His hands shake. He breaks it in half and gives half to Milo. They eat together in the cold room. Outside the window, for the first time in a thousand years, the snow stops.)*
>
> **Young Veyl:** *(barely a whisper)* What was her name? I forgot.
>
> **Milo:** Then we'll find it. That's what the Ardanes do.

# PART B — THE GAME, SCENE BY SCENE

PLAYED = you walk (left thumb / WASD, look with the right thumb / mouse, Use = E).
WATCHED = a cinematic.

## ACT ONE — CALDER ROW

### 1. The Room Above the Laundry — `ardane_01_home` — PLAYED
- **Where and when:** home, the one room above the laundry. Dawn, rain on the window.
- **Light:** one oil lamp by Nana's bed, warm (2000 K). Grey-blue daylight through the window. Steam from the laundry vent outside glows orange in the streetlight.
- **Mood:** tender, cramped, proud.
- **Sound:** rain, laundry machines below, a kettle, Nana humming the well-song.
- **Who and where:**
  - Nana in her bed by the window, red thread in her hands
  - Rosa at the small table counting coins into a jar
  - Bea on the floor drawing on the back of an eviction notice
  - Milo by the door
- **Beats**
  1. Fade in on the rain-streaked window.
  2. **Rosa:** "Forty-one. Forty-one and the rent is ninety."
  3. **Bea:** "I drew you, Milo. You're the big one."
  4. Milo walks to Nana (*Use*).
  5. **Nana:** "Come here, little lamp. Give me your wrist." She ties the red Knot.
  6. **Nana:** "Our family walked a long way to get to this room. One day you'll go and say hello to them."
  7. **Milo:** "Who, Nana?"
  8. **Nana:** "Everyone you come from."
- **Choice** (at the table, *Use*): **"Take the coins for bread"** or **"Leave them for Nana's medicine."** Remembered as *took the coins*. It changes one line in the epilogue.
- **Trailer frame:** Nana's hands tying red thread on Milo's wrist, the lamp behind them.
- **Next:** scene 2.

### 2. The Varen Arcade — `ardane_02_market` — PLAYED
- **Where and when:** the covered market of the Varens. Day, overcast, cold.
- **Light:** milky skylight, stall lamps, the gold glow from the Varen office windows above.
- **Mood:** busy, humiliating.
- **Sound:** a crowd, shouting traders, a busker's violin.
- **Who:** stall-keepers; Julian Varen leaning on the office stairs with two friends; Augustin Varen at the upper window.
- **Beats**
  1. Milo carries crates from the cart to the fish stall (*Use*).
  2. Julian blocks him: "Ardane. That's not even a real name anymore, is it? Your mother cleans our floors."
  3. Julian kicks a crate. Oranges roll across the stones.
  4. The crowd laughs. Augustin watches from above, unmoved.
- **Choice:**
  - **"Say it: 'My name is Milo Ardane.'"** Brave. Julian's friends grab him and he breaks free. Remembered as *stood up*.
  - **"Pick up the oranges in silence."** Proud. Julian sneers. Remembered as *kept quiet*.
  - **"Walk away."** Self-protective. Remembered as *walked away*.
- **After:** either way, Julian shouts "Thief! He took my watch!" (He didn't.) The guards turn. Milo runs.
- **Trailer frame:** oranges rolling across wet stones, a boy's hand reaching for one, a gold window above.
- **Next:** scene 3.

### 3. Thirteenth Alley — `ardane_03_alley` — WATCHED
- **Where and when:** the alley. Night, heavy rain, neon pharmacy cross, puddles.
- **Light:** sodium streetlight at the alley's mouth, green neon, then the portal's cold white-violet light.
- **Mood:** fear, then awe.
- **Sound:** rain, running footsteps, guards' shouts, a deep hum building to a roar, then silence.
- **Beats**
  1. Wide shot from the end of the alley: Milo sprints in, hood up.
  2. Close: he presses against the wall, breathing, counting ("one, two, three…").
  3. Behind him, a ring of light opens in the brick.
  4. His vision turns and swirls (the camera orbits faster and faster, then everything goes white).
- **Camera:** handheld chase, then a slow push in on the ring, then an orbit that becomes a spin, then a fade to white.
- **Trailer frame:** Milo small against the glowing ring, rain stopped mid-air around it.
- **Next:** scene 4.

## ACT TWO — VELASSE, THE WORLD OF WATER

### 4. The Drowned City — `ardane_04_velasse` — WATCHED
- **Where and when:** the same alley, now a narrow canal between stone towers. Dawn mist turning gold.
- **Light:** low gold sun through mist, lanterns still burning on the bridges.
- **Mood:** wonder.
- **Sound:** water, bells far off, gulls, an oar.
- **Beats**
  1. Milo wakes on a floating dock.
  2. A boat glides out of the mist. Nia, standing, oar in hand: "You're lucky. The Veyra boats would have run you over and charged you for the dent."
  3. **Milo:** "Where is this?"
  4. **Nia:** "Velasse. Where else? …You've got the Ardane look. Grandpa's going to cry."
- **Camera:** wide shot of the canal with towers in the mist, then close on Milo's face and Nia's silhouette.
- **Trailer frame:** Nia's boat cutting the gold mist between towers.
- **Next:** scene 5.

### 5. The Low Water — `ardane_05_canals` — PLAYED
- **Where and when:** the raft-village beneath the palaces. Morning, bright.
- **Light:** sun between towers, lanterns, the glitter on the water.
- **Sound:** water, children, a hammer, singing.
- **Who:** Old Ezio on his raft-workshop; raft families; House Veyra guards on the bridges above.
- **Beats**
  1. Walk the rafts (*Use* to talk).
  2. **Ezio:** "Ardane. My grandmother's grandmother told of an Ardane. Seren. She opened the Great Sluice when the flood came for us. They hanged her from it. Nobody up there remembers her. Down here we still light a lamp."
  3. **Milo:** "I don't understand how I'm here."
  4. **Ezio:** "Nobody understands how they're anywhere, boy. You live here too. Everyone on the rafts knows Milo of the Ferries. Quiet lad. Good with knots."
  5. A storm darkens the sky to the south.
- **Trailer frame:** lamps lit on the rafts in Seren's memory, under the palaces.
- **Next:** scene 6.

### 6. The Great Sluice — `ardane_06_sluice` — WATCHED
- **Where and when:** the sluice tower in the storm. Night, black rain, lightning.
- **Light:** lightning flashes, guards' lanterns, the green glow of the Tide Compass.
- **Sound:** roaring water, thunder, bells ringing alarm.
- **Beats**
  1. The flood is rising in the poor quarter. Doge Veyra has locked the gates to save his gardens.
  2. Milo climbs. Nia holds off the guards with her oar.
  3. **Milo**, at the wheel: "She did it. So can I."
  4. The wheel turns. The water roars into the noble gardens. The rafts rise safe.
  5. In the wheel's hub, a compass, green as sea-glass: the Tide Compass.
  6. Milo hears whispering. Names. Hundreds of names.
- **Choice:** **"Open it now"** (the poor are saved and the Veyra hunt him) or **"Wait for the bells"** (they wait, three rafts are lost, he opens it anyway). Remembered as *waited*. Nia remembers.
- **Trailer frame:** Milo silhouetted against the lightning on the iron wheel.
- **Next:** scene 7.

## ACT THREE — QADESH, THE WORLD OF SAND

### 7. The Red City — `ardane_07_qadesh` — WATCHED
- **Where and when:** the same alley, now a crack between sandstone walls. White noon.
- **Light:** hard sun, black shadows, heat haze.
- **Sound:** wind, distant bells, a market.
- **Beats**
  1. Milo and Nia stumble out of the portal into the light.
  2. Oren, a Sun guard, spear lowered: "Water-thieves. On your knees."
  3. **Nia:** "Do we look like we've had any water?"
- **Trailer frame:** two small figures in a red canyon of light.
- **Next:** scene 8.

### 8. The Palace of the Sunborn — `ardane_08_palace` — PLAYED
- **Where and when:** the palace courtyard. Evening, cool, fountains running.
- **Light:** torches, gold, the blue dusk sky.
- **Sound:** fountains, harps, whispering courtiers.
- **Who:** High Priest Ankh-Ra; courtiers; servants; Oren on guard.
- **Beats**
  1. Captured, Milo is set to serve wine at the feast while the city outside thirsts.
  2. **Oren**, quietly: "The king cries at night. Did you know that? He's afraid of the city. Fear builds walls. Walls need water."
